Paired 7/28-day compressive-strength model

30 mixtures × 2 curing ages · linear mixed model (random mixture intercept, age-specific residual variance) · corrected data set · generated 2026-09-28

Findings

Robust · Strength rises with SS to a peak at mid-range SS, at both ages
SS and SS² are the largest terms (p < 0.001, p < 0.001) and are kept in at least 99 % of re-selections. The peak lies at SS 58 % (7 d) and 61 % (28 d) for uncarbonated mixes at RCF 30 %, and lower (SS 46 % at 7 d, 38 % at 28 d) for carbonated mixes at RCF 50 %.
Robust · The 7 → 28-day gain is set by SS
f28/f7 = 2.92 (95 % CI 2.44–3.49) at SS 0 %, falls to a minimum of 1.45 near SS 55 % and is 1.58 (1.24–2.01) at SS 75 % (SS × Age p < 0.001). NaOH-only mixes gain most. SS is the only age-dependent variable in 86 % of residual-bootstrap and 91 % of subsample re-selections. The curvature of the gain (SS² × Age, p = 0.017) is moderate.
Moderate · Carbonated RCF helps in NaOH-rich mixes and hurts in silicate-rich ones
Carbonated × SS p < 0.001. At RCF 50 % and SS 0 % a 1 h carbonated mix is ×1.55 (95 % CI 1.18–2.05; p = 0.003) the uncarbonated one; at RCF 10 % and SS 75 % it is ×0.53 (95 % CI 0.40–0.72; p < 0.001). The crossover SS rises with RCF, from 5 % at RCF 10 % to 49 % at RCF 50 % (1 h). Kept in 97 % of residual-bootstrap but only 67 % of subsample re-selections: it rests on the 8 uncarbonated mixes.
Moderate · RCF raises strength when it is carbonated and when SS is low
Carbonated × RCF p = 0.024, RCF × SS p = 0.026. Going from 10 to 50 % RCF multiplies strength by ×2.08 (95 % CI 1.64–2.64; p < 0.001) for carbonated RCF at SS 0 %, and by ×0.91 (95 % CI 0.65–1.26; p = 0.55) for uncarbonated RCF at SS 75 %. RCF never lowers strength significantly. The case-bootstrap intervals of carbonated × RCF and RCF × SS include zero, so these two terms depend on a few mixtures.
Not detected · Carbonation duration (0.5, 1, 5 h): no difference detected
The three carbonated levels do not differ in level (p = 0.12); on/off coding fits about equally well (ΔAICc −1.13). Carbonation × age is not supported (p = 0.19). In the age-specific fit the 5 h level gains less than NC (×0.77, 95 % CI 0.58–1.02; p = 0.069), a single contrast that the joint test does not support.
Not detected · A/B ratio (0.42–0.48): no effect detected
No A/B term improves the model at either age (A/B p = 0.68, (A/B)² p = 0.29, A/B × Age p = 0.31). A/B curvature is selected only if the 28-day variance is forced equal to the 7-day variance, and does not survive nested cross-validation.
